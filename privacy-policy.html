Privacy Policy

App: Rick & Morty Explorer
Effective date: November 16, 2026

Rick & Morty Explorer is an informational application that lets users browse characters, locations, and episode information from the Rick and Morty universe. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not require an account or registration. It does not ask users to provide personal information, and the developer does not operate an account database for this app.

The app does not include advertising, Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or other in-app analytics or crash-reporting services.

2. Network Requests and Third-Party Services

The app connects to the public Rick and Morty API (rickandmortyapi.com) to retrieve character, location, and episode information. When your device makes these requests, the API provider and relevant network infrastructure may receive technical request information such as your IP address. Those services handle such information under their own policies and practices.

The developer does not receive or store this network request information through the app.

3. Location, Contacts, and Files

The app displays fictional locations associated with characters. It does not need your device's precise or approximate location, contacts, or personal files to provide its core features.

4. Data Sharing and Sale

The developer does not sell or share personal information collected by the app. The app sends the API requests described above so that requested content can be retrieved.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. It has no account or profile system and does not knowingly collect personal information from users.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this same URL, with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the app developer through the developer's GitHub profile: github.com/yousseftarekx2-tech.

Rick & Morty Explorer is an independent fan-made application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of the Rick and Morty franchise.
